Flintglen Rybbit

Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026. This policy covers the Google Search Console integration in Flintglen's private Rybbit workspace, operated by Flintglen.

Data we access

With your authorization, we access your list of available Search Console properties and read search performance reports for the property selected for a workspace site. Reports can include search queries, pages, countries, devices, dates, clicks, impressions, click-through rates and average positions.

Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on our controlled server so the workspace can retrieve reports without asking you to authorize every request. The requested Google scope is webmasters.readonly.

How data is used and shared

We use this data to display and retrieve search performance reports. Workspace access settings determine which members, authorized API integrations, or enabled public and shared dashboards can view a site's reports. An authorized integration may retrieve reports for a user-facing assistant or reporting workflow.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google's data is transmitted to our server and returned to the authorized report viewer or integration. Our hosting and network providers process data as needed to operate the workspace.

Storage, security and deletion

The workspace uses HTTPS and restricts administration of its server. Disconnecting Search Console in Site Settings deletes the stored connection and its OAuth tokens. You can also revoke access in your Google Account settings. This does not delete the source data held by Google.

Server backups may retain earlier connection records according to the operator's backup procedures. Contact us to request deletion of workspace-held Google data or ask about retained backups.

Google API Services

Flintglen Rybbit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact your Flintglen workspace administrator through your existing team contact. For public support, use the project's issue tracker without including private report data or credentials.
