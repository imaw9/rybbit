Terms of use

Effective November 16, 2026. Flintglen Rybbit is a private analytics workspace operated by Flintglen for its team and invited collaborators.

Authorized use

Use the workspace only with the operator's permission. Connect a Google Search Console property only if you are authorized to share its reports with the workspace members and integrations allowed by that site's access settings.

Keep your account credentials and API keys secure. Do not attempt to bypass access controls, access another team's data without permission, or disrupt the workspace.

Search reports

The Google connection is read-only. Google controls the availability, completeness and freshness of Search Console data; reports can be delayed or contain no results. The integration does not guarantee search rankings, indexing or traffic.

Ending access

You may disconnect a Search Console integration or revoke its Google authorization. Workspace administrators may change or remove workspace access. Google data handling is described in our privacy policy.

Contact and source

Contact your Flintglen workspace administrator for help, or use the public issue tracker for product support. This deployment uses an independently maintained Rybbit fork; its software license is available in that repository.
